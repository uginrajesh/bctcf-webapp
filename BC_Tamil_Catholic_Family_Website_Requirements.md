# BC Tamil Catholic Family Website Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

Create a modern, welcoming, bilingual community website for **BC Tamil Catholic Family**, a Tamil-speaking Catholic community based in British Columbia, Canada.

The website should serve as the primary online presence of the community and provide information about the community, upcoming events, prayer requests, new member registration, and social media channels.

### Audience
- Tamil Catholic families living in British Columbia
- New immigrants and newcomers
- Visitors looking for Tamil Catholic worship opportunities
- Community members seeking updates and announcements

### Desired Feel
- Warm
- Family-oriented
- Faith-centered
- Professional
- Simple and easy to maintain

---

## 2. Branding

## 20. Overall Design Goal

A welcoming online home for Tamil Catholic families in British Columbia, rooted in faith, family, fellowship, and service.

The website should combine:
- Catholic identity
- Tamil heritage
- Community unity
- Modern simplicity

without feeling commercial, corporate, or overly traditional.

## Non-Negotiable Design Principles
- Bilingual (Tamil + English) from day one
- Should be compatible in Mobile as well as in laptop or other devides
- Community-focused, not parish-focused
- Faith + Family + Tamil Heritage equally represented
- Low-maintenance content updates
- Prominent Prayer Requests and New Member onboarding
- Consistent use of the community logo and branding
- Warm, welcoming visual style rather than institutional church styling
